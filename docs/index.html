Underground Kingdom

Carve a home in the hostile deep.

Underground Kingdom is a dwarven colony sim set in a cold, dangerous cavern world. You are not a hero on a raid; you are the mind of a hall.

Dig tunnels, raise masonry, and turn a foothold of firelight into a working settlement while the wilderness stays dark, hungry, and close.

The cavern

Firelight against cave dusk

The wilderness stays dark, hungry, and close. Fog hides what you have not yet carved. The cavern is hostile; the settlement is warm — soot, stone, and antique metal, not comedy or candy magic.

You farm the cave’s edges. Unexplored dark waits beyond the last lantern. The mountain does not care if you are pretty. It cares if the hearth stays lit.

Colony and craft

The hall is a living settlement

Your dwarves eat, sleep, haul, cook, smith, and fight. You designate work — mining, building, farming the cave’s edges — and the colony executes.

Combat is real-time and local. Survive the first nights, then deepen the hall: storage, forges, doors, walls, meeting space. The long game is systems and logistics.

The same map

The dark lives next to your stockpiles

Combat is real-time and local. Giant Rats, Goblins, Orks, and Cave Trolls share the cavern with your stores, forges, and sleeping dwarves. Keep food, sleep, and safety — then survive the next threat.

  • Giant Rat

    Hunger that finds the food. Low, close, and already in the dark.

  • Goblin

    A raid on the same stone as your haul routes. Fast enough to punish an open door.

  • Ork

    Heavier than a goblin, still local. When they reach the hall, logistics becomes defense.

  • Cave Troll

    The cavern answering back. A body of stone and night against your firelight.

What you play

Systems over skirmish

The fantasy is atmospheric and grounded. Deep systems play over deathmatch. You keep a hall alive — you do not queue a deathmatch.

  • Dark-fantasy colony

    A dwarven hall in a hostile underground. No surface campaign. You are the mind of the settlement.

  • Dig the cave hall

    Designate mining and building. Tunnels, masonry, and a foothold of firelight that can grow.

  • Craft and logistics

    Storage, housing, forge, mine, fireplace, doors, walls, ladders, meeting hall. Haul, cook, smith, eat, sleep, build.

  • Defense in place

    Real-time combat in the same space as labor. Rats, goblins, orks, and cave trolls reach the stockpiles you failed to wall.

  • Dusk and hearth

    Cool cavern dusk against a warm, handcrafted settlement. Fog and unexplored dark beyond the last fire.

  • The long game

    Survive the first nights, then deepen the hall. The mountain cares if the hearth stays lit.

Core loop
