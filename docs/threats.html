The same map

The dark lives next to your stockpiles

Combat is real-time and local. Giant Rats, Goblins, Orks, and Cave Trolls share the cavern with your stores, forges, and sleeping dwarves.

Keep food, sleep, and safety — then survive the next threat. Damage is a kernel rule: actual equals max(0, raw − defense). The hall does not get a separate arena.

  • Giant Rat

    Hunger that finds the food. Low, close, and already in the dark.

  • Goblin

    A raid on the same stone as your haul routes. Fast enough to punish an open door.

  • Ork

    Heavier than a goblin, still local. When they reach the hall, logistics becomes defense.

  • Cave Troll

    The cavern answering back. A body of stone and night against your firelight.

This is Underground Kingdom — not UnderKingdom, not The Ants: Underground Kingdom, not a Steam listing yet. Pre-alpha. PC. Unity 6.
