Colony and craft

The hall is a living settlement

Your dwarves eat, sleep, haul, cook, smith, and fight. You designate work — mining, building, farming the cave’s edges — and the colony executes.

Buildings of the foothold

Survive the first nights, then deepen the hall: storage, forges, doors, walls, meeting space. The long game is systems and logistics — not a deathmatch queue.
